# 3-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00-0:25 Problem

TA and HR teams receive too many CVs during large trainee campaigns. CVs alone are weak signals, so strong candidates can be missed and HR spends too much time on manual screening.

Produck solves this by building the talent funnel earlier.

## 0:25-1:15 Student Funnel

Show the `Student funnel` view.

The candidate joins a free Product course before applying. Complete the three lessons. The system tracks learning progress. Then answer the readiness check and unlock the certificate.

This gives HR more evidence than a CV: completion, assessment score, engagement, and motivation.

Submit the application.

## 1:15-2:20 HR Command Room

The HR view now shows the live applicant inside the ranked queue with other sample candidates.

Open a top candidate. Explain that the AI Agent combines:

- Course completion
- Quiz score
- Engagement
- CV strength
- Motivation

Then show the recommendation: interview, case test, nurture, or hold.

## 2:20-2:50 Agent Coordination

Show the `Agent logistics plan`.

The system is not only ranking candidates. It also prepares the next recruiting action, such as sending a case test, reserving interview slots, notifying the hiring manager, or nurturing unfinished candidates.

Click `Run AI Agent` to show the queue can refresh.

## 2:50-3:00 Close

Produck helps HR convert large candidate volume into a smarter, evidence-based shortlist. The key idea is simple: do not wait for CVs only. Build the talent funnel earlier, track real signals, and let AI coordinate the process.
